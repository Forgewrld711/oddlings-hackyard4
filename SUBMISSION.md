# Oddlings — submission draft

## One-line pitch

A pet rock that hatches into a tiny roommate: small real-world chores furnish its home, without hunger meters, guilt or streak resets.

## Writeup

Oddlings turns ordinary tidying and preparation into a gentle creature-and-room game. Pick one of ten equally weighted stones, or pull a free mystery pick. Crack the stone, meet its resident, and choose one small non-food task: clear a landing pad, return a wandering object, or place something ready for later. Explicitly self-report completion to receive a bottle-cap stool, tiny shelf or basket for the room. Skipping never takes anything away.

The companion is yours without productivity requirements. Daily stars are unconditional, seven welcome visits need not be consecutive, and absence produces a little garden instead of a sad pet. Quiet company and reduced-motion controls keep the experience optional. A locally rendered share postcard excludes task details and hides the pet nickname unless the user opts in.

This entry runs as static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Progress stays in the browser; there are no accounts, live AI calls, tracking, payments or medical integrations. The creature's personality is authored fiction. A separate demo room lets reviewers simulate the loop without altering their real save.
